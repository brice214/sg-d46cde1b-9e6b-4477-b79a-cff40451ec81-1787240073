# LIONS SERVICES GABON - Corporate Landing Page

## Vision
Premium B2B landing page for an industrial inspection, control, and certification company operating in Oil & Gas, Marine, Bunkering, Mining, Chemical, Agriculture, and Import/Export sectors in Gabon. Target audience: operations managers, industrial decision-makers, procurement professionals.

## Design
**Reference:** International inspection bureaus (Bureau Veritas, Lloyd's Register) + Oil & Gas terminal interfaces — measured authority through technical precision.

**Color Palette:**
- `--primary: 217 100% 34%` (cobalt blue #0047AB from logo)
- `--navy: 210 100% 12%` (deep institutional #001F3F)
- `--gold: 43 65% 53%` (restrained accent #D4AF37)
- `--background: 0 0% 100%` (pure white)
- `--muted: 210 17% 98%` (cool tinted neutral)
- `--foreground: 0 0% 10%` (near-black)

**Typography:**
- Headings: Archivo (industrial geometric, weights 500/600/700) — precise, technical
- Body: Source Sans 3 (weights 400/600) — professional, readable

**Style Direction:** Asymmetric editorial compositions, large photography with navy overlays, generous whitespace, thin gold accent lines, strong vertical rhythm. Industrial precision without coldness — premium but operational.

## Features
Corporate landing page with sections: Hero, Positioning banner, About, Services (4 types), Philosophy, Independent Inspection, 6 Domains, Why Choose, Commitment, CTA, Contact form, Footer. All content in French. Client-provided photography for hero and key sections. Smooth scroll anchors, sticky header, mobile-responsive.
